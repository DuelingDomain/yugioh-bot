Yu-Gi-Oh! cube draft online

You can run a Yu-Gi-Oh! cube or booster draft in Dueling Domain: friends pick cards from packs on a timer, build a deck from their picks and duel in the same browser tab.

Join the waitlist

How a draft runs

The host creates a draft and sends an invite link. Two or more players can join. When the host starts, a short countdown runs and the first packs open. Each player picks a card, passes the pack on and picks again from the next pack. A pick clock keeps the draft moving.

When the last pick is made, your picks are your pool. You build a deck from that pool and duel with it in the same tab. There is nothing to export or print.

Options the host can set

  • How many cards are in each pack.
  • One pick or two picks per turn.
  • How many seconds each pick takes.
  • A limit of three copies of a card.
  • An optional Extra Deck draft, after the Main Deck rounds.

If the host stops a draft early, the draft is cancelled for everyone. Start a new one when your group is ready.

Build your own cube

A cube is a card pool that you control. You can make a cube from archetypes, or import one from a list of card names, a plain text file or a .ydk file. Your cubes are private to your account.

When you build a deck from your picks, you can also import a deck from a .ydk file, a ydke link or a list of names. The deck builder also shows Genesys points when you play that format.

Why draft here

A draft in person needs packs, sleeves and a lot of space. Online, the card pool is ready, the pick clock is fair to everyone and the result goes straight into a deck. After the draft you can duel in the same place. Choose a classic 1v1, a Tag duel or a 4-player game.

The alpha is free and invitation-only. Join the waitlist and we send your invite when your wave opens.

Questions and answers

How many players can join a draft?

Two or more players. The host sends an invite link and starts the draft with a short countdown.

Can I use my own card list as a cube?

Yes. You can build a cube in the editor, or import it from card names, a text file or a .ydk file. Cubes are private to your account.

What happens after the draft?

Your picks become your pool. You build a deck from it and duel in the same browser tab.

Is the draft free?

Yes. Dueling Domain is free to play. The alpha is invitation-only for now, so join the waitlist to get an invite.
