Points format

Genesys format online

Genesys is a Yu-Gi-Oh! format where each card has a point value and your deck must stay under a point cap, and the Dueling Domain deck builder counts the points for you.

Join the waitlist

How Genesys works

In Genesys, points replace the ban list. Most cards are worth zero points. A smaller set of strong cards carry points, and your deck can hold only so many points in total. You choose which strong cards fit under the cap.

In Dueling Domain, choosing Genesys turns the ban list off. The point cap is yours to set. The default is 100, and you can pick any whole number from 0 to 1000.

The points list

The deck builder uses the current points list for the TCG. A background job checks the list every week and updates the points when it changes.

The deck builder shows your running total while you build. If a deck goes over the cap, the deck builder flags it before you start a duel.

Standard or Domain

Genesys is a layer on top of the duel mode:

  • Genesys with the classic Standard game shows as Genesys.
  • Genesys with Domain Format shows as Genesys Domain.

Both work at every table size: 1v1, 2v2 Tag and 3- and 4-player free-for-all.

Save decks and play

You can save a Genesys deck with its point cap. The same deck can be imported from a .ydk file, a ydke link or a list of names. Then you create a game, choose the Genesys option and invite your friends.

The alpha is free and invitation-only. Join the waitlist and we send an invite by email when your wave opens.

Questions and answers

What is the Genesys point cap?

You choose the cap. The default is 100, and it can be any whole number from 0 to 1000.

Does Genesys use a ban list?

No. In Dueling Domain, Genesys points replace the ban list, and the ban list is turned off for those games.

Where does the points list come from?

The TCG points list is synced every week from a public card database, and the deck builder uses the latest accepted list.

Can I play Genesys with Domain Format or in multiplayer?

Yes. Genesys works with Standard or Domain at every table size. With Domain it shows as Genesys Domain.
