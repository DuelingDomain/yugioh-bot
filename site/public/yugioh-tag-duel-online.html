Yu-Gi-Oh! Tag duel online

Dueling Domain runs 2v2 Yu-Gi-Oh! Tag duels online in your browser: two teams of two, with one shared pool of Life Points for each team.

The Tag rules we use

A Tag table seats four duelists in two teams. The rules are written down, and the engine applies them for you.

  • One Life Point total per team. It equals the starting Life Points of both partners added together. With the default 8,000, each team starts at 16,000.
  • Shared field and Graveyard. "You control", "your field" and "your Graveyard" include your partner's cards. "Your hand" and "your Deck" mean only your own.
  • Facing duelists. Each duelist faces one opponent. The two who face each other share the Extra Monster Zones and the columns between them, in the same way as a 1v1 duel.
  • Direct attacks. You can attack directly only when both opposing duelists control no monsters. A monster of either opponent protects the shared Life Points.

Tag tables use Master Rule 5, like the other multiplayer tables.

Play it your way

Tag works with the classic Standard game, with Domain Format, and with Genesys points. Every duelist builds their own deck, so partners can bring different strategies.

To start, open Duels and choose Create game. Pick the Tag format, choose the rules and send the invite link. Friends join the seats, and each team has two. You do not install anything.

Why a browser Tag table helps

Tag duels are hard to set up in person. You need four players, a large table and someone who knows the shared-card rulings. In Dueling Domain the table is already set, the engine keeps count of the team Life Points, and each player sees their own hand.

If you want one big game with no teams, try 4-player free-for-all. Tell us when you think a Tag ruling is wrong.

Questions and answers

How many Life Points does a Tag team have?

Each team has one shared total. It is the starting Life Points of both partners added together, so a team starts at 16,000 with the default 8,000.

Can I use my partner's cards?

Your partner's field and Graveyard count as yours for "you control" and "your Graveyard". Your hand and your Deck stay private to you.

When can a team attack directly?

Only when both opposing duelists control no monsters. A monster of either opponent guards the shared Life Points.

Do I need a download or an account?

You need an account and an invite for the closed alpha. The game runs in your browser, with no download.
