4-player Yu-Gi-Oh! online

Dueling Domain lets you play 4-player Yu-Gi-Oh! online in your browser: four duelists at one table, each with their own Life Points, and the last duelist standing wins.

How a 4-player table works

A free-for-all table seats four duelists. Everyone starts with 8,000 Life Points by default, and nobody shares them. You win when every other duelist is out. Three-player tables work the same way with one seat fewer.

The tables open with a dice roll to decide who goes first. These tables use Master Rule 5, so the rules are the ones you know from the current game.

The engine knows about four players

Duels run on the open-source EDOPro rules engine. It was built for two players, so we extended it for three and four players and wrote rulings for the cards that need them.

  • A card that says "your opponent" asks you to choose one opponent. The other duelists are not affected.
  • "Your Life Points are lower than your opponent's" is true only when your Life Points are lower than those of every living opponent.
  • Cards that win the duel by themselves, such as Exodia, work. The owner wins and every other duelist loses.

If you think a ruling is wrong, tell us.

Start a 4-player game

Once you are in the alpha, open Duels and choose Create game. Pick the 4-player format, choose the rules and send the invite link to your friends. Each friend needs an account. Everyone plays in a browser tab, with no download.

You can also choose how the game plays. Domain Format and Genesys points both work at every table size, and so does the classic Standard game. Prefer teams? Read about 2v2 Tag duels.

Who it is for

Free-for-all is the format for a group of friends who want one big game instead of two small ones. It also suits streams and community nights, because every duelist has the same chance at the win.

The alpha is invitation-only. Join the waitlist and we send an invite by email when your wave opens. The game is free.

Questions and answers

Can I play 4-player Yu-Gi-Oh! online for free?

Yes. Dueling Domain is free to play. The alpha is invitation-only for now, so join the waitlist and we email your invite when your wave opens.

Is there a 3-player option?

Yes. Tables seat three or four duelists in free-for-all, two teams of two in Tag, or two duelists in a classic 1v1 duel.

Do I need to download anything?

No. You duel in a browser tab. It works best on a desktop or laptop with a modern browser today.

Does the game check the rules for me?

Yes. The engine allows only legal plays and resolves chains for you. For three and four players we extended it and wrote rulings for cards that name one opponent.
