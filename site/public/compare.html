Comparison

Yu-Gi-Oh! simulators compared

Dueling Domain is a free browser simulator in closed alpha that focuses on 3- and 4-player duels, Tag, drafts, Domain Format and Genesys; this page lists other ways to play, using only facts from their own pages.

Join the waitlist

How we wrote this page

We checked each product on 23 November 2026. We list only what its own page states. If a page does not say something, we do not guess. This page does not say that another product lacks a feature unless that product says so. Things change, so check each site for the latest.

Dueling Domain

  • Runs in a browser tab, with no download.
  • Free to play. The alpha is invitation-only, with a waitlist.
  • Table formats: 1v1, 2v2 Tag, and 3- and 4-player free-for-all.
  • Game modes: classic Standard, Domain Format and Genesys points.
  • Cube and booster drafts in the same place as the duels.
  • Duels run on the open-source EDOPro rules engine, which we extended for three and four players.

EDOPro

  • Describes itself as "the open-source automatic duel simulator".
  • Says it is available on Windows, Mac and Linux.
  • Describes itself as an evolution of the YGOPro system.

Source: the Project Ignis website, checked 23 November 2026.

Dueling Nexus

  • Says it is free.
  • Says it runs entirely in the browser, so there is no client to download.
  • Says it has unranked and ranked game modes and a deck editor.
  • Says chains, timing and priority resolve themselves.

Source: duelingnexus.com, checked 23 November 2026.

YGO Omega

  • Describes itself as a free simulator.
  • Says it runs through a launcher on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS.
  • Says every card is unlocked, ranked play is open and tournaments cost nothing.
  • Has automatic rules: it says the engine tracks life points, chains, timing windows and priority. It also has a manual mode.

Source: omega.duelistsunite.org, the official YGO Omega site, checked 23 November 2026.

Master Duel

  • The official video game. Its product page says it is free to play with in-game purchases.
  • Lists PlayStation 5 and 4, Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One, Steam, Nintendo Switch, iOS and Android.

Source: the official Master Duel product page, checked 23 November 2026.

Which one should you pick?

If you want an official game on a console or phone, Master Duel is built for that. If you want a program for Windows, Mac or Linux, EDOPro and YGO Omega are two. If you want a browser game, both Dueling Nexus and Dueling Domain run in the browser. Dueling Domain is the one to try when you want free-for-all and Tag tables, drafts, Domain Format or Genesys in the same place, and you can wait for an invite.

Dueling Domain is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with any product on this page.

Questions and answers

Is Dueling Domain free?

Yes. It is free to play. The alpha is invitation-only for now, so you join the waitlist and get an email invite when your wave opens.

Do I need to download Dueling Domain?

No. It runs in your browser. It works best on a desktop or laptop today.

Does Dueling Domain use the same rules engine as EDOPro?

Duels run on the open-source EDOPro rules engine. We extended it for three- and four-player tables and wrote rulings for them.
