Privacy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Who we are

Dueling Domain is an unofficial fan project. You can reach us at support@duelingdomain.com.

What we collect on this site

When you join the waitlist, we save your email address, which form you used, your browser's user agent and the time you joined. We use your IP address temporarily in memory to limit repeated submissions; we do not store it with your signup. Our server keeps logs for security, which may include IP addresses. They are kept for a limited time and then deleted automatically. We use no analytics.

Why we use it

We use your waitlist information to send alpha invites and alpha news about Dueling Domain only, and to keep the form working and prevent abuse. We do not sell your information, use it for ads, share it for targeted advertising or market anyone else's products.

Your consent

By submitting a waitlist form, you consent to these alpha emails. Consent is our legal basis for sending them. You can withdraw it at any time by emailing us to unsubscribe or delete your signup. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before you withdrew it.

Who processes your information

Hetzner hosts the site, the app and their databases on a server in Nuremberg, Germany. Clerk runs sign-in for the app. When you join the waitlist, we pass your email address to Clerk so we can approve you from there, and Clerk sends the waitlist and invite emails. These providers may process information outside your country; the protections available there may differ.

Russo One and Chakra Petch are served from our own host. Loading these fonts does not contact Google or another font provider.

In the app

The app uses Clerk for sign-in with email and password or, optionally, Discord. Discord sign-in shares your Discord ID, username and avatar. Clerk handles your password and sign-in details; the app keeps your email address, username and Discord ID with your account so it can recognise you. If you played before the alpha opened, we created your Clerk account from the email address Discord gave us, so you can sign in without a new invite.

The app also stores game data such as drafts, decks and matches so you can play and keep track of them.

Bug reports

When you use Report bug, we keep the report with your account. To track the fix, we also post the description you write and technical details (the page, duel state, screen size) as an issue on our public GitHub project. Names are removed from that issue, but anyone can read it, so do not put personal information in the description.

Cookies

The marketing page sets no cookies. The app uses strictly necessary sign-in cookies to keep you signed in and secure your account.

How long we keep it

We keep waitlist emails until the alpha ends or you ask us to delete them, whichever comes first. We delete your waitlist information within 30 days of a request.

We keep your account until you delete it. You can delete it at any time from Account settings in the app. That removes your sign-in, email address, saved decks and waitlist signup. Matches, drafts and tournaments you played in, and cubes you made, stay on the site under the name "Deleted player" so other players keep their history. Database backups are kept for 7 days, so deleted information is gone from them a week later. Public bug-report issues are not removed automatically; email us and we will remove them.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, get a copy, or withdraw your consent. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing and to data portability. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. We may need to verify that a request is yours.

Contact us to make a request or raise a concern. You can also complain to your local data protection or privacy authority.

Children

Dueling Domain is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are under 16 in the EU, you need a parent or guardian's consent. If you believe a child has shared information with us without the required consent, contact us and we will delete it on request.

Changes

We will update the date on this page when the notice changes. We will email the waitlist about material changes.

Contact

For privacy questions, to unsubscribe or to request deletion, email support@duelingdomain.com.
