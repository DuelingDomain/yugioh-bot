Before you sit down

Questions at the table.

Straight answers about the game and the alpha.

Is Dueling Domain free?

Yes. Dueling Domain is free to play and nothing in it is for sale. The alpha is invite-only for now: join the waitlist and we email your invite when your wave opens.

Can I play Yu-Gi-Oh! online in my browser?

Yes. You duel in a browser tab, with no download and no client. Today it works best on a desktop or laptop with a modern browser.

Can I play Yu-Gi-Oh! with three or four players?

Yes. Free-for-all seats three or four duelists, each with their own Life Points, and the last duelist standing wins. Tag duels put two teams of two at one table, and each team shares one pool of Life Points. Classic 1v1 duels are there too.

Are the rules automated?

Yes. Duels run on the open-source EDOPro rules engine, so only legal plays are allowed and chains resolve for you. For 3- and 4-player tables we extended the engine and wrote rulings for cards that name one opponent. Tell us when you think a ruling is wrong.

Can I play Domain Format online?

Yes. Every duelist keeps a Deck Master in its own zone, at any table size, and the engine runs the Domain rules for you. Domain Format is a community format; we are not affiliated with its organizers.

Does it support Genesys?

Yes. Genesys decks use the current TCG points list, and the deck builder shows your points total. You can also pick a current TCG or OCG ban list, or play with none.

Can we draft a cube with friends?

Yes. Run a cube or booster draft for your group. Packs pass around the table on a pick clock, your picks become your pool, and you build a deck and duel with it in the same tab.

Is Dueling Domain an official Yu-Gi-Oh! game?

No. Dueling Domain is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of the game. Rights holders can email support@duelingdomain.com.
