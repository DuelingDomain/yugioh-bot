Domain Format online

Domain Format is a community Yu-Gi-Oh! format where each duelist keeps a Deck Master in a zone of its own, and Dueling Domain runs those rules for you in the browser.

Join the waitlist

What changes in Domain Format

Domain Format adds one idea to the normal game: the Deck Master. It is a monster that starts outside your decks, in its own zone. You can summon it, and you can return it to its zone later.

Domain Format is a community format. Dueling Domain is not affiliated with its organizers. We follow the written rules and tell you when we are not sure.

Deck rules we enforce

  • The Main Deck has exactly 60 cards and the Extra Deck has at most 15.
  • Main Deck and Extra Deck are singleton. You can have one copy of each card, including alternate art.
  • There is no Side Deck.
  • The Deck Master is separate from those counts. It cannot also be in your decks.
  • There is no ban list unless the host chooses one.
  • The cards in your deck must belong to your Deck Master's Domain. That includes its archetypes, Attributes and Monster Types, and the ones its effect text names. Spell and Trap cards have no Domain restriction.

The cost of using a Deck Master

Using a Deck Master costs Life Points. The first time it leaves its zone is free. After that, the cost is 500 Life Points for each time it has already returned to the zone. The engine keeps the count, so you do not have to track it by hand.

Every table size

Domain works in a 1v1 duel, in 2v2 Tag and in 3- and 4-player free-for-all. You can also combine it with Genesys points. The table then shows as Genesys Domain.

The deck builder checks your deck against these rules before you duel. You can import a deck from a .ydk file, a ydke link or a list of card names.

Questions and answers

What is a Deck Master?

A Deck Master is a monster that you keep in its own zone, apart from your Main Deck. It can leave that zone and return to it. The first leave is free, and later leaves cost Life Points.

How big is a Domain deck?

The Main Deck has exactly 60 cards and the Extra Deck has at most 15. Both are singleton, and there is no Side Deck.

Can I play Domain Format with three or four players?

Yes. Domain works at every table size: 1v1, Tag, and three- or four-player free-for-all.

Is Dueling Domain the official home of the format?

No. Domain Format is a community format and we are not affiliated with its organizers. We run its rules in the simulator.
