About Dueling Domain

Dueling Domain is a free, fan-made Yu-Gi-Oh! simulator that runs in your browser, built by the Dueling Domain team for friends who want automated duels and drafts together.

Join the waitlist

What you can play today

Dueling Domain puts the whole evening in one tab. You can run a cube draft, build a deck from your picks and duel with friends. Duels cover a classic 1v1, 2v2 Tag and 3- and 4-player free-for-all. You can add Domain Format or Genesys points at any table size.

Duels run on the open-source EDOPro rules engine, so only legal plays are allowed and chains resolve for you.

Who it is for

Dueling Domain is for groups of friends, local communities and streamers who want to play together without a table, a stack of packs or a rules expert nearby. You can also play a single duel against one friend, and the same tab handles deck building. It works best on a desktop or laptop with a modern browser today.

Access: a closed alpha

The alpha is free and invitation-only. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee access. We send invitations by email in waves. The app needs a sign-in, and its private pages are not part of this public site.

Unofficial fan project

Dueling Domain is not an official Yu-Gi-Oh! game. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the owners of the game. If you are a rights holder and you have a concern, email support@duelingdomain.com.
