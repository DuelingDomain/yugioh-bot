core: in Tag a monster of the partner is a Synchro material of the team, at n > 2

Owner rule [R-TAG-PARTNER-COST] (ADR-0002): "You may use your partner's cards for costs, Tributes, materials and summon conditions."
card::is_can_be_synchro_material refused a monster on the field when its controller was not the controller of the Synchro Monster
(raw compare, the stock rule that only the own monsters are material, unless EFFECT_SYNCHRO_MATERIAL). The Lua filter of the stock
procedure (proc_synchro.lua synchmatfilter) accepts `mc:IsControler(tp) or mc:IsCanBeSynchroMaterial(c)`, so a Tuner or a
non-Tuner of the partner was never a material. With more than two duelists the test is now "same team" (both are duelists and
share a team). Free for all has no partner: same_team is false for two different duelists, so nothing changes there. With two
duelists the stock compare is kept. EFFECT_SYNCHRO_MATERIAL (any field) is unchanged.
