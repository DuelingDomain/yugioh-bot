# Multi-player duels use Domain Format rules for free-for-all and official TCG Tag rules for 2v2

**Status:** accepted; updated 2026-10-04 by the product owner. Standard and Domain FFA3 and FFA4 use the [Domain Format rules](https://www.domainformat.com/rules) and the [TCGplayer Domain Format article](https://www.tcgplayer.com/content/article/How-To-Play-Domain-Yu-Gi-Oh-s-New-Multiplayer-Commander-Format/84ab787e-a192-4882-a721-3a71d57c650e/). Tag keeps the official TCG Tag rules. A rule marked "(pending engine change)" is a decided rule that the current engine does not yet implement.

We are adding three multi-player formats next to the current 1v1 duel: **2v2 Tag**, **3-player free-for-all** and **4-player free-for-all**. All three work in both Standard and Domain duels. Tag, FFA3 and FFA4 allow only Master Rule 5. Master Rules 1 to 5 are available only for 1v1. The old-rule duel flags `OATH_OLD`, `ONCE_OLD_NEGATE` and `1_FACEUP_FIELD` stay refused at 3-4 seats. Each duelist has their own main field. In FFA4, seats across from each other share the Extra Monster Zones and columns, as specified below. The EDOPro shared-field tag (one field and one LP total per team, partners swap each turn) was considered and rejected.

Konami publishes official rules only for Tag Duels ([TCG, revised 5 December 2019](https://www.yugioh-card.com/en/play/tagduel/)). The free-for-all rules below follow Domain Format, with the owner decisions of 2026-10-02. The first-draw rule below also applies to 1v1.

## Rules common to every multi-player format

- `[R-FFA-FIRST-DRAW]` **First draw by duel mode and Master Rule (all seat layouts).** Tag, FFA3 and FFA4 use only Master Rule 5: in Domain every duelist draws on their first turn, including the turn-1 duelist; in Standard only the turn-1 duelist skips the draw. In 1v1, Master Rules 1 to 5 are allowed. In 1v1 Domain, every duelist draws on their first turn at every Master Rule. Known deviation: the legacy 1v1 engine (`DUEL_1V1_ENGINE=legacy`) skips the Domain turn-1 draw at MR3-MR5; the saved `firstTurnDraw` flag records the actual behavior. In 1v1 Standard MR1 and MR2, the turn-1 duelist draws, as specified by the stock core flags. In 1v1 Standard MR3, MR4 and MR5, only the turn-1 duelist skips the draw. MR3 removed the turn-1 draw on 21 March 2014. The duel creator shows: "In 1v1 Domain, the duelist who goes first draws at Master Rule 1 and 2; at Master Rule 3 to 5 this depends on the server engine. The second duelist always draws." The rule id is kept for existing references.

  **Known deviation, accepted by the owner on 2026-10-02:** The default legacy 1v1 engine keeps the stock Master Rule draw rule in Standard and Domain. In Domain MR3, MR4 and MR5, the turn-1 duelist does not draw. This is an accepted exception to the Domain draw rule. The pinned engine follows the Domain draw rule. The saved `firstTurnDraw` flag records the actual engine behavior; it is `false` for legacy Domain MR3 through MR5.

- `[R-COMMON-SEP-FIELDS]` **Separate fields.** Each duelist has their own Main Monster Zones, Spell & Trap Zones, Field Zone, Pendulum Zones (per the Master Rule), Graveyard, banishment, hand, Deck and Extra Deck. In Domain, each duelist has their own Deck Master and Deck Master zone.
The FFA rules for "your opponent" are in the free-for-all section. Tag uses the opposing team under its official rules.
- `[R-COMMON-CONTROLLER-TURN]` **Your duelist's turn.** An effect or maintenance cost that applies during "your" Standby Phase, Battle Phase or End Phase uses the turn of the card's current controller. This is one real duelist in every format. In Tag, only that duelist's turn qualifies; the partner's turn is a separate turn. A maintenance cost paid with LP still uses the shared team LP under R-TAG-LP. A card that explicitly names its owner uses that owner as its text requires.
- `[R-COMMON-OPP-PICK]` **"Opponent" for hand, Deck, Extra Deck, draws and LP.** When an effect affects an opponent's hand, Deck or Extra Deck, makes an opponent draw, or targets an opponent's LP, the activating duelist **picks one opponent when they activate it**. The pick is part of the activation. An informational hint does not ask for an opponent or bind one. An opponent hint goes to every living opponent; in Tag it does not go to the partner. In Tag, a "during your opponent's turn" condition does not select the opponent. For example, for Elfnote Tinia the activator picks the opposing member at activation; the turn player is not bound (owner, 2026-10-03 early morning).
- `[R-COMMON-CTRL]` **Damage to the controller.** When card text damages "the controller" of a card, or "you" as that card's controller, the duelist who controls that card when the effect resolves takes the damage. No other opponent takes this damage. In Tag, the controller's team LP takes the damage. The owner approved this rule on 2026-10-02.
- `[R-COMMON-ONGOING]` **Continuous face-up effects.** A continuous effect applies to all players or cards that meet its requirements. A continuous restriction on "your opponent" applies to all opponents, except Swords of Revealing Light in FFA (see R-FFA-SWORDS-PROTECT). A lasting lock created by an activated effect in FFA follows R-FFA-ACTIVATED-LOCK.
- `[R-COMMON-EACH-PLAYER]` **"Each player" / "both players" / "neither player".** "Each player" and "both players" mean every living duelist, including the Tag partner. "Neither player" means no player. For resource changes, each duelist acts in turn order, starting with the turn player. The Law of the Normal (66926224) can activate only if the activator has another card in hand and at least one opponent has a card in hand. At resolution, every living duelist discards their hand, including the Tag partner. An empty hand does nothing.
- `[R-COMMON-ALL-BOTH]` **"All" and both sides of the field.** An effect that affects "all" cards, or cards on both sides of the field (for example Dark Hole: "destroy all monsters on the field"), affects every duelist, you and your 2v2 partner included. An effect on "your opponent" affects only opponents, never your partner.
- `[R-COMMON-EMZ]` **Separate Extra Monster Zones (FFA3 and Tag).** Each duelist has their own two Extra Monster Zones (EMZ). FFA3 columns are separate too. Under Master Rule 5, as in 1v1, a duelist may use one EMZ, and may use the second only when the 1v1 rules allow it. Different duelists' EMZ do not block each other. FFA4 uses R-FFA-ACROSS-EMZ (pending engine change).
- `[R-COMMON-FL-LIST]` The Forbidden & Limited List applies per duelist (per Deck).
- `[R-COMMON-SEAT-STATE]` **Per-player flags and counters (owner decision Q6).** A flag or counter that a card keeps for each player (for example Curse of the Circle, Wiseman's Chalice, Fatal Abacus) has one slot per seat in free-for-all and one slot per team in Tag. The key is the team in Tag and the seat in free-for-all (rule R2 of the triage).
- `[R-COMMON-RETURN-TO-OWNER]` **Effects that return a card to its owner.** In every duel format, including 2v2 Tag, an effect that returns a card to its owner's field, hand, Deck, Extra Deck, Graveyard or banishment uses the real owner seat. The controller and the player who applies the effect do not change that destination. Apply the card text for the destination and the fallback when the return is not possible. This rule does not replace `[R-FFA-RETURN-OWNED-CARDS]`, which applies only to elimination. A control effect gives the card to the seat that the effect names. In FFA, 'your opponent' means the bound opponent of that effect, and 'you' means the effect's controller. When the effect ends, the remaining control effects decide the controller; with none, the card returns to its owner. An effect that returns a card to its owner gives it to the real owner's seat (R-COMMON-RETURN-TO-OWNER). If that owner is eliminated, the card stays with its controller; only tokens can be in this state (R-FFA-ELIMINATION). 1v1 and Tag keep the stock rules.

- `[R-COMMON-SURRENDER-EOT]` **Immediate surrender (owner rules, 2026-10-03 and 2026-10-04).** The id name is historical; the rule is immediate. Keep this id for existing test and coverage references. FFA3 and FFA4 surrender takes effect immediately on any player's turn. There is no surrender queue and no wait for the End Phase or turn end. With no open chain, the loss lands when the surrender is accepted. The leaver's cards leave the board, ongoing effects stop, and future turns skip that seat. The next prompt of a living duelist cannot precede the loss. A living duelist's current choice stays open, except for the response window to a departed turn player defined below.

Return cleanup keeps the leaver's automatic answer and a living duelist's submitted answer, with the prompt's selection inputs, until the suspended processor resumes. If cleanup opens a required choice for a living duelist, that choice is answered first, then the saved answer resumes.

Engine decision under R-COMMON-SURRENDER-EOT: if surrender changes a counter offer, the engine asks again with the current sources when they can pay the full count, otherwise an effect removal returns false without removing counters or raising its event, and a REASON_COST removal stops the suspended cost script and cancels its pending summon before placement, using the core's failed-summon cleanup without refunding earlier completed payments (owner may override).

An unfinished activation or summon of the leaver is cancelled if no Chain Link is open. Its removed card cannot return to the board. The engine consumes the leaver's open choice with its automatic answer so that the cancelled operation can stop. A living duelist's opponent pick keeps its option ids, but an option for a removed seat is no longer legal when a living option remains.

With an open chain, the host flags the surrender immediately, even when the chain is between two other duelists. The current chain resolves fully, then the loss lands before any further action prompt. Every Chain Link resolves normally, including the leaver's unresolved links and a link that already started to resolve. Surrender alone does not negate a link or its effect. Other loss reasons still use R-FFA-ELIMINATION. Until the chain finishes, the leaver's other cards stay on the board and ongoing effects still apply. The engine answers prompts held by the leaver with a pass when possible, or its existing deterministic choice when a required choice has no pass. Those answers cannot block the duel. Living duelists keep their choices during the chain. The host stops the leaver's clock and rejects further answers from that seat. The view sets pendingElimination only for this short chain window and clears it when the loss lands or the duel ends.

If the leaver was the turn player and the duel continues, the next living seat starts a new turn, including its Draw Phase. No End Phase card effect of the leaver runs. A cut-short turn still counts as an ended turn: the core runs its End Phase event for living duelists and applies the turn-count resets described below. With no open chain, an optional response window of a living duelist closes if it responds to an action by the leaver or an action that involves the leaver. A window that concerns only other living duelists stays open with the same prompt and option ids. The leaver leaves immediately in both cases. In the second case, the next turn waits until that window and any resulting chain finish. A required choice of a living duelist stays open.

The core records each event's participants when it raises that event, before later moves change the cards. It combines these records when it opens a response window. Single-card events keep the same records until all responses to that action finish. Optional trigger activation choices, including Yes/No choices, follow this window rule. Required choices made inside a living effect stay open. Participants are the action reason player, a meaningful event player, and the owners and controllers of the action source and event cards. An affected movement or control event also names the card's controller before that move. A shuffle of Set cards within the same field does not add a controller from an older move. Battle participants include the attacker and defender. Literal zero values in events that do not name a player do not add seat 0. SSET has both real-player and literal-zero writers, so its affected cards name its subject. A rule-only event without a reason effect does not name an action reason player. A synthetic effect handler with no current or previous location is not an action card. Phase, pre-draw, free-chain, Adjust, break-effect, chain-end and turn-end events do not add action participants. A window with no action participants stays open when the turn player leaves. The cut-short turn opens no later empty phase window. If a real action and a phase event share a window, the real action decides its participants. The test is made before the leaver's cards are removed. This rule applies only to FFA turn-player surrender with no open chain. With an open chain, living response windows remain open and the whole chain resolves first. After the chain resolves and the turn player's surrender loss lands, a living duelist's optional trigger whose event involves the leaver closes under the same window rule (owner rule, 2026-10-04). R-FFA-NO-ATTACK still applies. The last living duelist wins when all other living duelists surrender. There is no special surrender queue order. Losses flagged together during one chain use the core's normal loss processing.

Tag surrender with no open chain loses the whole team immediately. Tag surrender with an open chain ends the duel at once, without resolving that chain. 1v1 surrender still ends the duel at once. All formats use "Surrender" as the result reason and in new elimination logs. Old saved "Surrendered" results stay readable. Time-limit losses keep their existing timing and fallback.

After a loss lands while an FFA duel continues, the player can explicitly ask to spectate (owner decision, 2026-10-04). Without that request, the host keeps the player role and own seat. A living or Leaving seat cannot spectate and receives HTTP 409. A spectator view hides private cards and sets mySide and prompt to null. After the duel completes, every seated player sees their own result, role and seat, including players in earlier loss groups and players who spectated during the duel. This also applies when the request asks to spectate. An interrupted duel keeps the same own-player flow. FFA draw seats stay eliminated and keep the player role. Tag draws have no seat-loss message. Room membership and seat records stay for results and replay. If an interrupted duel has no final board, a recorded loss command or saved surrendered seat for that seat (or its Tag team) permits a replay check for a loss that landed. A chain loss that did not land keeps the player role. If replay is unavailable and there is no final board, the room stays available with the player role. An explicit replay request still returns its error.

The journal records each accepted loss command as eliminate:0 at its revision. Recovery and replay apply that command at the same point and repeat the same internal automatic answers. Retired eliminate-eot commands are identified and refused; this engine cannot reproduce their old turn-end timing. The saved final board remains available.

New multiplayer surrender requires Debug.SurrenderDuelist and Debug.EliminateDuelist. The surrender function checks pending surrenders immediately without settling unfinished LP, Deck or effect-win losses, or advancing a pending time-limit loss. It defers removal while the current chain is open. A core with only the old flag function cannot meet the immediate rule while another duelist holds a prompt. The host returns HTTP 409 for an unsupported core, saves no loss command, and starts no automatic answers. Install the immediate-surrender core before retrying. This requirement does not apply to 1v1 surrender or Tag surrender with an open chain, which the host ends directly.

## 2v2 Tag (official TCG Tag Duel rules)

- `[R-TAG-LP]` Two teams of two duelists. **Each team shares one LP total**, equal to the sum of its members' starting LP (16,000 with the default 8,000).
- `[R-TAG-ORDER]` Turn order is 1A, 2A, 1B, 2B. First draws follow R-FFA-FIRST-DRAW. The first three duelists cannot attack; the first Battle Phase is turn 4.
- `[R-TAG-SHARED-CARDS]` "You control", "your field" and "your Graveyard" include your partner's cards. "Your hand" and "your Deck" mean only your own.
- `[R-TAG-UNIQUE]` **Unique cards.** In Tag, a unique-on-field limit ("You can only control 1") permits one copy per team. Both team fields count.
- `[R-TAG-ATTACK]` **Direct attacks.** In Tag, you may attack a duelist directly when that duelist controls no monster, even if the partner controls monsters. This is an exception to R-TAG-SHARED-CARDS.
- `[R-TAG-PARTNER-COST]` You may use your partner's cards for costs, Tributes, materials and summon conditions. You cannot activate your partner's cards or effects.
- `[R-TAG-PARTNER]` Your partner is not your opponent. Your effects on "your opponent" do not affect your partner, and a card that works only on an opponent's card (for example Effect Veiler or Infinite Impermanence) cannot be used on your partner's card. A card that negates an activation, an effect or a summon (for example Solemn Judgment, Solemn Warning or Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring) cannot negate your partner's activation, effect or summon. It can negate the activations and summons of the opposing team, and your own, as in 1v1.
- `[R-TAG-VISIBILITY]` Partners may see each other's hands and Set cards.
- `[R-TAG-RESPONSE]` After a Chain Link, the opposing team gets the first chance to respond. Simultaneous triggers resolve in the order turn player, turn player's partner, then the opposing team.
- `[R-TAG-LOSS]` A team loses when its LP reaches 0, or when either of its duelists must draw from an empty Deck.
- `[R-TAG-TURN-COUNT]` Turn-count effects (for example Final Countdown) count every duelist's turn.

## 3-player and 4-player free-for-all (Domain Format rules)

These are the defaults. The organizer may later get options for LP.

- `[R-FFA-LP]` Each duelist has their own LP (default 8,000).
- `[R-FFA-ORDER]` Turn order is clockwise in seat order. First draws follow R-FFA-FIRST-DRAW.
- `[R-FFA-NO-ATTACK]` **The last duelist gets the first Battle Phase on their first turn (pending engine change).** In FFA3 this is turn 3; in FFA4 this is turn 4. Turns 1 through n-1 have no Battle Phase. Only living duelists count: a duelist eliminated before their first turn does not keep this window open. The window ends in the turn when the last living duelist starts their first turn.
- `[R-FFA-OPP-ONE]` **"Your opponent": one declared opponent (pending engine change).** A condition that does not change resources needs only one opponent to meet it. For an activated or ignition effect on "your opponent", declare ONE opponent at activation. Pick the opponent first, then any cards. The effect applies only to that opponent, including their field, Graveyard and banishment. Raigeki and Harpie's Feather Duster affect one declared opponent. Book of Eclipse and Prediction Princess Astromorrigan also use one declared opponent; their later End Phase effects affect only that opponent. An effect with "either GY" text, such as Monster Reborn, declares no opponent; its target choice is a card choice. It can target a monster in the Graveyard of any living duelist.
- `[R-FFA-OPP-RESPONSE]` **Trigger and response effects (pending engine change).** An effect on "your opponent" applies only to the opponent or opponents that met its requirement. A Mirror Force-type response to an opponent's monster declaring an attack is open to every duelist for whom the attacker is an opponent. If A attacks B, C may activate Mirror Force, but it affects only A's monsters. A direct-attack response, such as Battle Fader, Gorz, Drowning Mirror Force or Number 100: Numeron Dragon, is offered only to the attacked duelist (in Tag, the attacked team). Abyss Actor - Liberty Dramatist goes to the field of the opponent whose monster or LP the declared attack targets (owner, 2026-10-03 early morning). In FFA, a phase trigger that names no opponent's turn declares one opponent that meets its condition. A trigger in "your opponent's <phase>" binds the turn player with no pick. If the saved causal opponent leaves and an availability check fails, the queued link stays on the chain. It uses its count limit and completes with no effect. No cost is paid when the failed check stops the cost callback. Other players may respond to the link. This is an engine decision; owner can change it.
- `[R-FFA-SWORDS-PROTECT]` **Swords of Revealing Light is legal in FFA.** Face-up Swords protects only its controller and that controller's monsters from attacks. Other opponents can attack each other. It flips every opponent's face-down monsters. It leaves the field after three living opponent turns under stock R3. This exception applies only to Swords. Tag and 1v1 keep the stock card rule. If a defender is eliminated before a redirect to a direct attack resolves in FFA, the fallback uses ascending seat order. It selects the lowest living opposing seat that permits a direct attack and skips seats protected by Swords. A living defender remains the defender; if Swords protects that seat, the redirect fails.
- `[R-FFA-ACTIVATED-LOCK]` **Lasting locks from activated cards (pending engine change).** A lock that an activated card creates on "your opponent", such as "your opponent cannot ... this turn", applies to ONE opponent declared at activation. Face-up continuous effects still follow R-COMMON-ONGOING.
- `[R-FFA-DECLARED-DURATION]` **Declared-opponent duration (owner decision, 2026-10-03).** In FFA, this rule applies only to player effects (owner, 2026-10-02 late). A player effect is an effect registered on a player with `Duel.RegisterEffect` (`EFFECT_FLAG_FIELD_ONLY`). After a player effect binds ONE declared opponent, a duration such as "until your opponent's next turn" (`RESET_OPPO_TURN` without `RESET_SELF_TURN`) counts only that opponent's turns. Time Seal skips that opponent's next Draw Phase. Appointer of the Red Lotus returns the card during that opponent's next End Phase. SINGLE, EQUIP and FIELD effects registered on a card keep R3, even when they bind one declared opponent. Swords of Revealing Light keeps R3: each opponent's turn counts (owner, 2026-10-02 night). Player effects with no declared opponent also keep R3 below. If the declared seat is eliminated, the duration changes to R3 from that point: each living opponent's turn counts. The owner approved the eliminated-seat fallback on 2026-10-02 late. 1v1 and Tag do not change.
- `[R-FFA-LOCK-CONTROL-CHANGE]` **A declared opponent takes the source of a continuous lock (pending engine change).** In FFA, only the source card's own continuous locks, active while it is face-up, apply to the old controller (the seat that lost the card) when the declared opponent takes control of the source card. A lasting lock already registered on a player stays on that player, as in 1v1. Other FFA opponents stay free. Tag keeps the opposing-team rule. See the owner answers in `DECISIONS-2026-10-01.md`, 2026-10-02 evening (rule) and 2026-10-03 (scope).
- `[R-FFA-RESOURCE-ROTATION]` **Resource changes between duelists (pending engine change).** Each living duelist acts in turn order, starting with the turn player. For Creature Swap-type effects, every living duelist must control a monster and chooses one. Each chosen monster goes to the next living duelist in turn order; each duelist receives the monster of the previous living duelist. The moves form one rotation, including when all Main Monster Zones are full.
- `[R-FFA-ACROSS-EMZ]` **Shared Extra Monster Zones and columns in FFA4 (pending engine change).** Seats across from each other, 0/2 and 1/3, share two EMZ and columns as in 1v1. A monster in one EMZ blocks the matching EMZ of the seat across. Link arrows and column effects use that across seat. FFA3 keeps separate EMZ and columns.
- `[R-FFA-CHAIN]` **Chain responses (owner, 2026-10-04; Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4, "Turn proceeding").** After a duelist adds a Chain Link, the next living duelist in clockwise turn order gets the first chance to respond. Responses continue clockwise, with the activating duelist last. Each new link starts a fresh pass round after its activating duelist. The chain resolves when every living duelist passes in a row after the last link. Eliminated and leaving seats are skipped. Example: A activates, B passes, C chains; D responds first, then A, B and C. This applies to FFA3 and FFA4, in Standard and Domain. Open game state, SEGOC and simultaneous trigger ordering keep their existing rules.
  - Example (4 players, player 1 is the turn player): player 1 activates a card. Players 2, 3, 4 and then 1 may respond. Player 2 chains. Player 3 responds first, then players 4, 1 and 2.
- `[R-FFA-TRIGGERS]` **Simultaneous triggers** go on the chain in turn order: the turn player first, then clockwise.
- `[R-FFA-NEGATE]` A card that negates an activation, an effect or a summon (for example Solemn Judgment or Ash Blossom & Joyous Spring) can negate the activation, effect or summon of any duelist, as in 1v1.
- `[R-FFA-ATTACK]` The attacking duelist picks any opponent's monster, or makes a direct attack on an opponent who controls no monster.
- `[R-FFA-ELIMINATION]` A duelist loses at 0 LP or when they must draw from an empty Deck. **Their cards leave the game**, including their cards that another duelist controls, and their ongoing effects stop. Seats do not move. Tokens that the loser owns on another duelist's field stay (pending engine change). **Their Chain Links that are already on the chain resolve with no effect.** For losses other than surrender, this holds from the moment the loss is flagged: the loss lands after the chain, but the Chain Links of that duelist must not act. A surrender is an exception: its links resolve normally and its loss lands after the whole chain (R-COMMON-SURRENDER-EOT). This sentence is only about Chain Links. Until the chain ends, the other cards of a flagged duelist stay on the field and their ongoing effects still apply. A Chain Link that has already started to resolve when its duelist loses finishes with its effect, because the check is made once, when the link starts to resolve.
- `[R-FFA-RETURN-OWNED-CARDS]` **Return cards owned by other duelists after elimination (pending engine change).** A card the loser controls but another duelist owns returns to the owner's zone of the same kind, with its position kept where applicable. Cards in the loser's hand, Graveyard or banishment return to the owner's same location. If a card cannot return to that zone, it goes to the owner's Graveyard.
- **A turn cut short by an elimination counts as an ended turn** for every turn count (for example `RESET_OPPO_TURN`): when the turn player loses in its own turn, a card such as Nightmare's Steelcage ends exactly as if that turn had reached its End Phase.
- `[R-FFA-WINNER]` The last duelist left wins.
  - If the last duelists left reach 0 LP at the same time (for example one effect that damages all of them), nobody is left: the duel is a **draw** and has no winner (`winnerSeat` is null). If one effect takes all but one duelist to 0 LP, the one duelist left wins.

## Decisions added on 2026-09-30 (product owner)

- The FFA chain response decision from 2026-09-30 is superseded by the owner's 2026-10-04 confirmation of Domain Format Complete Rulebook v1.4: respond clockwise after the latest link's activating duelist (R-FFA-CHAIN above). 2v2 keeps the official Tag rule (the opposing team responds first); 1v1 is unchanged.
- FFA3 and Tag keep separate EMZ. The 2026-10-02 FFA4 across-seat rule replaces the earlier separate-EMZ decision for FFA4.
- The Chain Links of an eliminated duelist resolve with no effect.
- A partner is never "your opponent". "All" and both-side effects include the partner.
- Negation and partners: in 2v2 Tag, a card that negates an activation, an effect or a summon cannot negate the partner's. In free-for-all it can negate any duelist. Your own activation stays as in 1v1.
- `[R-COMMON-CONT-NEG]` Continuous negation and lock effects (for example Jinzo, which negates all Traps) apply to every duelist at every table, the partner included. The partner rule above is only for a card that negates one activation, effect or summon.

## Card decisions (2026-10-01, product owner)

- **"You win" cards are forbidden in free-for-all and in Tag.** The deck check refuses all 17 cards that end the duel with a plain win: True Exodia, Relay Soul, Exodius the Ultimate Forbidden Lord, Holactie the Creator of Light, Number iC1000: Numerounius Numerounia, Exodia, the Legendary Defender, Ghostrick Angel of Mischief, Number C88: Gimmick Puppet Disaster Leo, Flying Elephant, F.A. Winners, Summer Schoolwork Successful!, Vennominaga the Deity of Poisonous Snakes, Jackpot 7, Destiny Board, Musical Sumo Dice Games, Phantasm Spiral Assault and Number 88: Gimmick Puppet of Leo. Exodia the Forbidden One, Final Countdown and Last Turn were already forbidden, so every script that calls `Duel.Win` is now on the list.
- **Kaiju and Lava Golem: the card goes to the field of the player whose monster was Tributed.** This holds for every card that has the "Tribute a monster of an opponent, Special Summon to that field" procedure: the 7 Kaiju (Gameciel, Radian, Kumongous, Gadarla, Thunder King, Jizukiru, Dogoran), Lava Golem, Volcanic Queen, Alien Skull, Santa Claws, Surgical Striker - H.A.M.P. (for its opponent-field procedure), and The Winged Dragon of Ra - Sphere Mode (added 2026-10-01: all Tributed monsters come from ONE opponent, and it goes to that field). Lava Golem must Tribute 2 monsters of the same opponent. In Tag, the Tributed monster belongs to an opposing member, so the card goes to that member's field. The Kaiju summon with no Tribute needs a Kaiju on the field of any opponent and goes to your own field.
- **Pineapple Blast and Evenly Matched: one opponent in free-for-all, joined fields in Tag.** In free-for-all, the activating player picks ONE opponent when they activate it (R-COMMON-OPP-PICK). The card compares you with that opponent's field only, and only that opponent's cards are affected. That opponent chooses their own cards, as in 1v1. In Tag, the fields of the two opposing members are joined: the count uses the cards of both together against the cards of your own team. The picked opposing duelist makes the choice from the joined field (one chooser, see question 5 below). This is the rule for all count and compare cards (question 2 below).
- **A summon to the field of an opponent: the summoning player picks one opponent.** This holds for every card whose Special Summon puts the card, or its tokens, on the field of an opponent. Examples: Jormungardr the Nordic Serpent, Fenrir the Nordic Wolf, Grinder Golem and Fallen of Argyros (summon procedures), and the cards with an effect that summons to an opponent's field (for example the Ojama token cards). The pick is made when the player summons. In Tag, the opponent is an opposing member. The Kaiju and Lava cards keep the Tribute rule above. The Winged Dragon of Ra - Sphere Mode is not in this group: it follows the Tribute rule (question 8 below). The pick shows only an opponent who can receive the monster. In Tag, an opposing member who cannot receive it is not shown (owner, 2026-10-02 late).
- **The other cards use the defaults.** Messenger of Peace: the 100 LP is paid only in your own Standby Phase (in Tag, the Standby Phase of your own duelist turn, and the team LP pays). Royal Tribute: every opponent discards the monsters in their hand, and in Tag 'both players' means every duelist, the partner included. Soul Exchange: you may Tribute 1 monster of any opponent. Snatch Steal: the owner of the monster gains the LP, in the own Standby Phase of that owner. In FFA, Book of Eclipse and Prediction Princess Astromorrigan now affect ONE declared opponent (2026-10-02, pending engine change). This replaces the 2026-10-01 rule that made each opponent flip and draw, or take damage. Tag keeps the per-member Book of Eclipse result and its shared-LP Astromorrigan result.

### Answers to the ten triage questions (2026-10-01, product owner)

The triage of 457 cards that needed a product decision is in `.status/multiplayer-triage.md`. The owner answered its ten questions. Questions 2 and 5 are answered with a different rule than the one proposed there.

1. **Turn count (Q1): option (a), rule R3.** In FFA, effects that do not follow R-FFA-DECLARED-DURATION count every turn of any living opponent as one opponent turn. Only player effects registered with `Duel.RegisterEffect` (`EFFECT_FLAG_FIELD_ONLY`) and bound to one declared opponent follow R-FFA-DECLARED-DURATION in the FFA rule list. SINGLE, EQUIP and FIELD effects registered on a card keep R3. Player effects use R3 if no opponent was declared. If the declared opponent is eliminated, player effects change to R3 from that point. In Tag, only a turn of the two opposing duelists counts. About 30 cards use `RESET_OPPO_TURN` with a count of 2 or more. Effects that keep R3 get shorter in rounds (N/2 rounds with 3 players and in Tag, N/3 rounds with 4 players). The review `.status/multiplayer-turncount-review.md` rated all 30 cards and 24 more cards with an own counter: **no new bans**. Some cards are on a WATCH list (stronger but fair, for example Double Payback, Magical Spring, Memory of an Adversary, Steel Scorpion, Extinction on Schedule, Clock Tower Prison, Life Shaver, Vain Betrayer). They stay legal. **The 4 free-for-all turn-count bans stay**: Doom Virus Dragon, The Wicked Avatar, Grisaille Prison and Million-Century Ice Prison. Destiny Board is already forbidden (alternative win). R3 is not the same as `[R-TAG-TURN-COUNT]`, which counts every duelist turn, own team included.
   **Swords of Revealing Light is legal in FFA.** It follows R-FFA-SWORDS-PROTECT below. The four other turn-count bans remain.
2. **Count and compare cards (Q2): changed meaning.** "Your opponent controls more ..." and the cards that compare field, hand or card counts (about 50, for example Evenly Matched and Pineapple Blast) do **not** add all opponents together. In free-for-all, the activator **picks ONE opponent** when they activate the card (bind), and the card compares with that opponent only. In Tag, the card compares with the **combined** field or hand of the two opposing duelists (the opposing team). This replaces the proposal "add all opponents together". In FFA, the 2026-10-02 R-FFA-OPP-ONE rule also applies to other activated effects on "your opponent". In FFA, a compare that is only a condition and changes no resource declares no opponent (for example Re-Cover). One opponent that meets the condition is enough (owner, 2026-10-02).
3. **"Each player" (Q3): accepted.** "Each player" is every living duelist, the Tag partner included (rule R1, `[R-COMMON-EACH-PLAYER]`).
4. **Duel-style cards (Q4): accepted.** Ante, Dragged Down, Dice Jar, Reversal Quiz, Trading Places and the other cards of this kind (about 80) act on you and one picked opponent. The other opponents do nothing. Tag uses the team LP.
5. **"Your opponent chooses" (Q5): changed meaning.** When the card text says "your opponent" in the singular, **ONE opponent chooses**: the picked or bound opponent, in free-for-all and in Tag. Only when the card text says "all" or "each" opponent do all of them choose. This replaces rule R4 of the triage ("each affected opponent chooses from their own cards"). The core design rule is the same: `Select*(1-tp)` makes the bound opponent choose.
6. **Per-player flags and counters (Q6): accepted.** The key is the team in Tag and the seat in free-for-all (rule R2).
7. **Swap of control (Q7): accepted.** The activator may pick a monster of any opponent. A targeted swap is between one of your own monsters and that monster. Creature Swap-type effects in FFA now use R-FFA-RESOURCE-ROTATION (2026-10-02, pending engine change). Mirror Gate needs a script fix (see question 10).
8. **Tribute of an opponent monster (Q8): accepted, with more.** The "Tribute a monster your opponent controls" effects (`EXTRA_RELEASE`, rule R5) work for the monsters of any opponent. This is a core fix: the engine found only the monsters of seat 1 before. It includes the Kaiju, Lava Golem and **The Winged Dragon of Ra - Sphere Mode**. For Ra Sphere Mode, all Tributed monsters come from ONE opponent, and the monster goes to the field of that opponent. Ra Sphere Mode therefore follows the Kaiju and Lava Golem rule (the Kaiju bullet above). It does not follow the rule "the summoning player picks one opponent". The earlier text put it in that group and said it Tributes your own monsters. That was wrong.
9. **Defeated duelists (Q9): accepted.** "Each player" effects skip a duelist who has lost, as `[R-FFA-ELIMINATION]` says.
10. **New bans (Q10): none.** The owner said "make those fixes now". Five cards get a per-card script fix: Contract with Don Thousand, Tsumuha-Kutsunagi the Lord of Swords, Infernoid Tierra, Two-for-One Team and Mirror Gate (it needs a check that the attacked monster belongs to the activating duelist). Two cards get a label fix, because the label must keep the real seat: Curse of the Circle and Wiseman's Chalice. The 4 turn-count bans above are not new.

These cards stay legal in the deck check. Their rules are listed in `MULTIPLAYER_CARD_RULES` (`packages/duel-server/src/banlists/multiplayer.ts`). `engine: "native"` means that the engine does the rule and a live scenario proves it for that card (the proven tables are in `proven`). `engine: "pending"` means that no live scenario proves it for that card yet. The details, and the list of proven cards, are in `docs/specs/2026-09-30-multiplayer-card-scenarios.md`.

## Domain deck rules (2026-10-02)

Domain uses exactly 60 Main Deck cards and at most 15 Extra Deck cards. Main and Extra are singleton by card identity, including alternate art and treated-as names. The Deck Master is separate from these counts and cannot also be in the decks. There is no Side Deck and no banlist by default; the host may choose a banlist, and the multiplayer safety list still applies.

The Domain includes the Deck Master's archetypes, Attributes and Monster Types, and those mentioned in its effect text. A specifically named card is allowed, and its archetypes are included. Flavor text does not add Domain membership. Divine Attribute and Divine-Beast are in every Domain. Spell and Trap Cards have no Domain restriction.

## Retired rule ID

- `[R-COMMON-OPP-FIELD]` **Retired on 2026-10-02.** This ID records earlier scenarios for the old rule that an opponent-field effect hits all opponents. It is not an active FFA rule, and those scenarios do not prove R-FFA-OPP-ONE. The new rules above replace it. Tag retains the official opposing-team field rule.

## Open points for the engine test

- The app flow for a team response window in 2v2: both partners see their own options, the team passes only when both pass, and the first activation becomes the Chain Link.
- Which cards need a per-card rule because their text does not fit the Domain Format errata. The automatic card test suite (ADR-0003) finds and tracks them.
- Patch 0084 follow-up L3: save the turn player before the prompt for a trigger in "your opponent's <phase>". If that player leaves, the trigger must keep that seat under R-FFA-OPP-RESPONSE.
- Patch 0084 follow-up Natural Disaster (`c18158397`): derive its opponent from the destroyed cards in the event group. The owner must decide how to handle an event group with cards from more than one opponent. Both follow-ups remain open.

## Consequences

- The EDOPro core supports only two sides. Separate fields for 3 or 4 duelists need core changes; the engine design is a separate decision (see `docs/roadmap.md`, step 1).
- The app model moves from "two seats" to "N duelists in teams": LP per team, one winner or a winning team, N clocks, per-seat privacy with a partner rule, and a multi-field layout.
