## Representative Set paths

- **Toy → Ashtra:** Toy Box Sets Toy Soldier, Toy Tank or Toy Magician as a Spell. Yoa the Plague Deity Ashtra, Mikumari the Sealing Deity Ashtra or Kuzuha the Bewitching Deity Ashtra can reveal and return that actual Set resource to pay its hand Summon procedure. Toy Box cannot Set an Ashtra card directly. Toy → Argostars remains absent.
- **Labrynth → Ashtra:** Lady Labrynth of the Silver Castle, Lovely Labrynth of the Silver Castle, Arias the Labrynth Butler, Farewelcome Labrynth and Labrynth Set-Up can Set qualifying Ashtra Normal Traps through their printed origins and conditions. Arias retains its scoped same-turn permission. Labrynth Cooclock also permits a Normal Trap this turn with its printed Labrynth-control condition, paired with Yoa’s checked Deck Set. These lines are alternatives.
- **Lady of Lament → Ashtra:** Lilith, Lady of Lament reveals three Normal Traps before the random Set; Malice, Lady of Lament and Loris, Lady of Lament Set qualifying Normal Traps from their printed GY/banishment origins. Their costs, triggers and leave-field clauses remain requirements.
- **Sunvine → Argostars:** Sunvine Shrine Sets an Argostars Continuous Trap from the GY during the opponent’s End Phase.
- **Supreme King → Argostars:** Miracle of the Supreme King Sets the Quick-Play Spell Argostars - Giantslaying from the Deck after its printed requirements.

An unrestricted any-card, any-Spell/Trap or any-Trap toolbox does not establish access alone. A real named/archetype or narrower subtype filter does. Sangen Furo’s banish-on-leaving recipient cannot pay Ashtra’s required return to hand. Recipient exclusions, separate Spell/Trap choices, attack-only Traps, trap-monster restrictions and Normal/Continuous activation scopes have catalog integration coverage. Generic unsupported multi-body Extra Deck setup remains excluded.

## Exact response additions

Current projections return at most three alternatives, including an empty array when none survive. Each line has one to three exact evidence card names; matching ordered steps have captions up to 500 characters. These paths were included in the pair review; they are not independent per-path verdicts. Previously accepted evidence is retained without appending newly discovered, unreviewed alternatives.

The reason payload additionally supports `alternative?: true` for other recipients of an already scored action (no aggregate score increase), and `setup?: {card: number; using: number; capability: "face_down_card"}` for the complete Set-resource hand Summon procedure. Presentation paths and captions stay outside judge inputs/hashes and shared or subscriber-specific query keys. No files in `packages/web/src/components` were changed; there are no new visible controls or rendered sections in this build.
