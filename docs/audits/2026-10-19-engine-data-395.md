## Smoke comparison repair

The original four CI smoke shards stopped before playing a card: the old row-only snapshot applied current remap overrides but had no history interval in which to validate the removed preview sources. The comparison now passes an empty override map for that temporary old snapshot. It preserves the installed candidate and its validated remaps, including admission and saved-deck migration. The real comparison now completes and selects its changed/helper-sample cards. The smoke conservation check now subtracts the initial inventory owned by an eliminated FFA player. The turn-order checker permits a player who loses during the new Draw Phase to finish that turn's phase boundaries, as ADR-0002 requires. It still checks the next living turn and refuses a free action on an eliminated turn.

CI also exposed an invalid smoke setup for Dragunity Divine Lance: a face-up Equip Spell was injected without an equip target. Equip Spells now enter through hand or set cases so the engine establishes a legal equip relation. Uncovered effects remain WARN. The snapshot round-trip check now compares its separate Pendulum fields with the matching MR5 zones.
