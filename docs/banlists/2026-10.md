## Source conflicts and the delayed change

- **Droll & Lock Bird:** the retrieved Konami Part 1 says **Newly Limited**. The published Konami list, Ignis and Yugipedia agree. The task brief reported Forbidden; the implementation follows the current primary sources (1 copy).
- **Perfectron Hydradrive Dragon (13203964):** Konami Part 1 and the published list say **Limited**, effective **2026-11-11** with Magnificent Maestros. The task brief reported Forbidden. Ignis already includes the future limit; October omits it. The separately selectable `tcg-2026-11-11` snapshot adds the 1-copy limit. October remains pinned; switching the default on November 11 requires an explicit follow-up change to `PINNED_TCG_BANLIST_ID`. No clock-dependent mutation changes a stored duel. The card-pool/release checks remain independent.
- Konami Part 3 writes “Sayuja Skull Dread”; the published full list, Ignis and Yugipedia use **Saryuja Skull Dread (74997493)**.
- Spelling-only source differences: Ignis “Agido the Ancient Sentry” / Konami and Yugipedia “Agido the Ancient Sentinel”; Ignis “Trap Dustshot” / “Trap Dustshoot”; Ignis “Divine Wind of the Mist Valley” / “Divine Wind of Mist Valley”; YGOrganization Part 2 “D/D/D Wave High King Ceasar” / “D/D/D Wave High King Caesar”. Passcodes resolve the same cards.
- After normalizing those names and Yugipedia’s semantic `Maliss P/Q` names, there are no status or card-membership disagreements among the full Konami, Ignis and Yugipedia snapshots, apart from excluding the delayed Perfectron entry for October.
