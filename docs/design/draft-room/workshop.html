The clock runs round the table

The pick clock is a line of light round the edge of the table, taken from concept B. It starts full and drains clockwise from the far side. A spark marks where it's burning.

  • Your turn. Warm white, like the lamp.
  • Ten seconds left. It turns gold.
  • Five left. It turns red and pulses, and the lamp dims a little.
  • You've picked. It dims. It's the table's clock now, not yours, so it stops nagging.
  • Everyone's in. The whole edge flares gold, then goes dark while the packs move. For the next pick it lays itself back round the table.

The number in the top bar stays for the exact time.

Why. You can feel the time running out without taking your eyes off the cards. The flare gives the table a shared moment every time the last person picks.

Friends at the table

Everyone has a seat around the table, with their name, their theme in a theme draft, and their pack in hand. The pack shows how many cards are left in it.

A light comes down over anyone still picking. When they pick, the light goes out and the seat says Picked. You can see who the table is waiting on without reading a list.

Why. A cube night is about the people as much as the cards. The seats make the draft feel shared, and they answer the most common question at the table: who are we waiting on?

The pass

Once everyone has picked, every pack moves at the same time. Each friend's pack slides to the next seat. Your leftovers gather and slide to your neighbour, and a new pack lands in front of you.

The direction shows in the top bar and on the table edge: left in packs 1 and 3, right in pack 2.

Why. The pass is the heartbeat of a real draft. Showing it makes the table feel alive and tells you where your leftovers went.

A room that's lived in

Small things keep the table moving between picks. Each one is tied to what's happening at the table.

  • The lamp. It hangs over the table and sways a touch. Dust drifts through its light. It dims for the last five seconds of your pick.
  • Friends deciding. Three dots under their name, like someone typing. When they pick, they nod as their card goes down.
  • Your pack. Now and then the lamp catches a card.
  • Table talk. Friends chime in at real moments: gl when a pack opens, no way when it comes back thin, gg when a pack ends, lol when the clock picks for someone. They use the same fixed lines you do. The mock fakes them; in the app every line comes from a real friend.

Calm turns off everything that loops: the sway, dust, dots, glints and the sparks. The clock line still drains. Off stops it all.

Why. A game night is never still. These touches are small, so the room feels lived in without getting in the way of the pick.

What you've got, at a glance

The tray at the bottom is your side of the table. The dial shows how many cards you have, and its ring shows the mix. Each slot shows a count and your latest card of that kind.

Monsters Spells Traps Extra deck

Tap a slot to filter by that kind. On smaller screens, tap the dial to open your picks. The same four colours run through the tray, the binder and the end screen.

When the draft ends, the end screen shows the final mix and a plain note about your pool, such as how many main deck monsters need no tribute.

Your picks, searchable

The binder on the right holds everything you've picked. Search names, types and card text. Press / to jump to the search box from anywhere.

Open Breakdown to filter by:

  • Monster level, shown as a small curve (1–4, 5–6, 7 and up)
  • Monster type, such as Dragon or Spellcaster
  • Attribute
  • Spell and trap type, such as Quick-Play or Counter
  • Archetype

Sort by type, with copies merged, or in the order you picked them. Each row shows the card's type line and stats. Point at a row to read the full card on the left.

On smaller laptops the binder slides in as a drawer. On a phone it's a sheet you open from the tray.

Filters light up the pack

A filter doesn't only narrow your picks. It also works on the pack in front of you. Matching cards glow and the rest dim, with a note such as 2 of 8 in this pack: Monsters.

So "show me spells" answers two questions at once: what spells do I have, and which spells can I take now?

Clear it from the note on the table or from the binder.

What's been drafted so far

When a pack comes back around, a note says how many cards are gone since you last saw it, with small pictures of them.

The Taken by others tab collects every card that left a pack you saw, with the pack and the pick it was gone by. It uses the same search and filters as your picks.

This tells you what your friends are drafting. If three dragons vanished, someone is on dragons.

Table talk

The Say button sends a quick line to the table: gg, lol, nice, hurry up, no way, gl. It shows as a bubble over your seat for a few seconds.

Friends' lines show over their seats. On a phone they show in the seat strip in place of the name, so they never cover the table.

Lines are fixed, so there's nothing to moderate. Full chat stays in Discord, where the group already is.

Animations, your call

The Animations button in the top bar has three settings:

  • Full. Holograms, the lamp and its dust, table lights and card movement.
  • Calm. Cards still move when you pick and pass, and the edge still flares. Nothing loops.
  • Off. Nothing moves. Changes happen at once, and the clock line steps once a second.

It starts on Off if your device asks for reduced motion, and on Full otherwise. Your choice is remembered in this browser. In the app it could be saved to your profile so it follows you.

Theme drafts too

In a theme draft nobody passes. You get a private pack of three each round from your own theme pool, which sits beside the pack on the table.

Seats show each friend's theme. The top bar shows whether you're on the main deck or the extra deck, and the pool's card back changes for the extra deck.

What it takes to build

Most of this runs on data the app already has. One filter needs data the app doesn't store yet. Here it is first, so you know exactly what you're approving.

Monster type, and spell and trap type

The mock filters by monster type (Dragon, Spellcaster) and by spell and trap type (Quick-Play, Counter). The app doesn't store either today. The card catalog has no column for them, and the draft API doesn't send them. There are two ways to get them:

Add it to the card catalog

Save the type when cards sync from YGOPRODeck. Clean and fast. It needs a new column in schema.ts, which is on the do-not-touch list and overlaps PR #53.

Read it from the duel engine

The engine's card data already has monster types and spell and trap types. No schema change, but the draft API would depend on engine data.

Search leans on the same data. In the mock, typing "dragon" finds Dragon-type monsters. Without it, search matches names and card text only, so a Dragon whose text never says "dragon" won't show up.

Until one of these lands, the room can ship without those two chip rows. Everything else still works.

Kind, attribute, level, search

Works today

The draft API already sends name, type, frame, attribute, level, card text and stats. That covers the tray, the level curve, the attribute chips, and search on names and card text.

Archetype filter

Small API change

The card catalog already stores archetype. The draft API just needs to send it with each card. No schema change. Search by archetype name comes with it.

Edge clock

Works today

The room already checks the draft every second for the seconds left and the clock length. The current timer bar runs on those, and the line can too. No server change.

The lamp, dust, dots and glints

Works today

Browser only. The thinking dots use the same picked or not picked state the seats already have.

Taken by others

Works today

Worked out in the browser by comparing a pack with what you saw last time round. No server change. It only knows about packs you've seen, which is also all a real player would know. It lives in the browser, so a reload mid-draft empties it unless it's saved there too.

Friends' seats

Works today

The room already knows whether each player has picked. That's all the seats need. Pack counts follow from the pick number.

Table talk

New live event

Needs one new event on the live draft server so a line reaches everyone at the table. Fixed lines only, with a short cooldown.

Animations setting

Works today

Saved in the browser. Moving it to the user's profile later is optional.

One question

Should friends see each other's picks while the draft is running? Today picks stay hidden. Other players only see that you've picked, not what.

Keep picks hiddenLike a real table. You read the table from what goes missing. This is what the mock does.
Show picks liveMore banter and more "you took my card". It also makes reading the table too easy.

My pick: keep them hidden while drafting, and show everyone's pools on the end screen. You get the reveal and the gg moment without spoiling the draft.
