Solid Vision

The tournament page is a duel field drawn in light. It shows every match being played, puts your duel first, and fills in as duels finish.

Your night on one field

You stand at the near edge of a duel field and your opponent stands at the far edge. The field between you is drawn in light, with your half in violet.

Your row of five zones is your five rounds. Each zone shows how that round went or what's coming:

A win is a gold locator card A loss is a face-down card The current round glows Upcoming rounds show who you play

A locator card carries the monogram of the player you beat. Your opponent's row at the far edge works the same way, so you can see how their night is going.

Every table, live

Every table in the round sits in a strip across the top, so you see them all straight away. Your table comes first. Each card shows both players, the game score, the best of 3 marks and a status such as Game 2 in progress. Under the strip, your own match is the big field.

A tournament from a 12 player draft has six tables. On desktop they wrap into rows. On a phone they sit in one row that scrolls sideways inside itself, and the page never scrolls sideways.

A 12 player round robin has 11 rounds. Your field shows five of them: two before the current round, the current one and two after. A small counter at each end holds the rest, such as "Rounds 1 to 3: 2 wins". The mock has five rounds, so it shows them all.

Watch any duel

Anyone can watch a game in progress, whether they're in the tournament or not. A table only gets a Watch button while a game is being played. Between games there is no button, and you never see one on your own match.

If you're not playing this round, there is no big field. Every table shows at the same size, and each one also shows the two players' rounds as small cards, so you can see how their night is going.

In the mock, Watch only shows a small note that says "Opens the duel to watch."

Straight into your duel

Before your match starts, the main button under your seat says Start duel. Once a game is in progress, or between games, it says Open duel. The middle of your field says which: "Game 2 in progress" or "Between games. Game 3 next."

The buttons sit under your seat, next to what you gain or lose with a win or a loss. Tournament games use the deck you registered, so there's no deck step.

Results come from the duel

The duel system sends each finished game to this page live. Nobody types a score in. When a match ends, this happens:

  • The score on that table rolls to its final value.
  • A gold locator card resolves out of the table like a hologram, from a thin line of light.
  • It flies to the winner's row in the standings. If it's your match, it lands in your round zone on the field instead.
  • The table says Final and its Watch button fades out. Rows re-sort, and the results feed gets a new line at the top.

When one game ends and the match goes on, only that table's score rolls and it reads "Between games."

When a result is reported by hand

Under your buttons is one quiet line: "Played off the site? Report a result." It's a backup for games that weren't played in the duel room.

When someone reports, the other player sees the score in a gold frame. The page says, for example, "Kestrel reported by hand that they won 2 to 1." Confirm and Deny sit under your seat.

If they do nothing, the result confirms itself after the confirm window. The mock shows 23 hours.

The champion

When the tournament ends, the field becomes the champion's field. Only the far half is lit, in gold, and the champion's five rounds all hold gold locator cards.

Across the middle it says who won the tournament, with their record. Standings below show the final ranks. Back to the draft is the only link left.

Single elimination

The field has three zones: Round 1, Round 2 and the Final. Under it is your road, a path of three stops. Each stop holds that round's matches as pairs of monograms with scores, your path is traced in violet, and a bye is a single chip that says Bye.

Round 2 isn't drawn until round 1 ends. Until then the page says "Round 2 is drawn when Kestrel and duel.josh finish." Once it's drawn, a bye reads "Bye this round. You go straight to the final."

The live round 1 match still has its Watch button.

Before it starts, and host tools

In the lobby only your half of the field is lit. Their half is a dashed outline that says your first opponent is drawn when the host starts. Below the field, "Who's in" lists everyone with Deck in or No deck yet, and a Leave button on your own row.

The invite link has a Copy link button, and players can also join from Discord with /tournament join. Someone who hasn't joined sees a Join the tournament button.

As host, the lobby has a row with Start the tournament, Add a bot, Announce in Discord and Cancel the tournament. Once it's live, Host tools opens a drawer with everyone's deck status, End now and Cancel the tournament.

Each red button says what it does. End now leaves unplayed matches unplayed and records no champion. Cancel the tournament removes it for everyone, results and Elo included.

Animations, your call

The Animations button has three settings:

  • Full. Your current round zone breathes, the field's glow drifts very slowly, live dots pulse, and the result moment plays in full.
  • Calm. The result moment is shorter and simpler. Nothing loops.
  • Off. Nothing moves. Changes happen at once.

It follows the device's reduced-motion setting, so it starts on Calm if your device asks for less motion. Only transform and opacity move, so nothing is slow to draw.

On a phone

The field lies flat. Your row and their row of five zones sit one above the other, with the deck zone above each row.

A sticky bar at the bottom holds your duel buttons: Start duel or Open duel, or Confirm and Deny when a result needs you. The tables row scrolls sideways inside itself, so the page doesn't.

Host tools opens a full-height sheet. Below the field come the standings, then the rail's sections: Waiting to confirm, Results and Rules.

So there's no deck step before a duel.
