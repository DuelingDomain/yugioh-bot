FFA table: using the whole screen round 2

Three ways to give the 4-player table the room it already has, without losing what it looks like. Each concept keeps the plaza floor, the glowing zones, receding rival boards, holo LP plates, the turn ring, the hand row, the phase bar and the shared Extra Monster Zones. What changes is where things sit and how big they get. Every page below is live; every size is measured from the rendered page, not estimated.

Game state for every shot: you are Aster (8,000). Rook to the west has 5,400, Juniper across has 3,100 (down 1,200 from 4,300), and Mirelle to the east has 6,600. It is turn 7, Main Phase 1.

Risks and build notes

These point at the real files under packages/web/src/components/duel/. Nothing in the app was changed for this workshop.

  • The stage is the real limit. table/geometry.ts fixes STAGE = 1100×860, and stageFit() returns min(w/1100, h/860). table/table-stage.tsx (line 76) applies it, so on any screen wider than about 1.28:1 the table is height-bound and leaves width unused. All three concepts need a stage width that follows the box (for example 1100–1500) and poses written as functions of that width, not the fixed FFA4_HOME / FFA3_HOME numbers.
  • Plate anchors. ffa4Anchor / ffa3Anchor in geometry.ts are fixed stage-px corners. The mock docks each plate to its own board's projected corner, then checks it against every other board with a polygon test. Today's vN plate already overlaps Rook's board at 1920 and 1440; it is the only audit flag in the recreation. table/holo-lp.tsx needs a corner-relative anchor.
  • Compact chips. compactFor() switches rival fields to chips under a 44px card height (40px under 1440 wide). Wide and Arena keep rivals above that at every tested size. Today's 1440 table also draws full boards, so chips stay a fallback for small windows.
  • Upright side labels. Wide depends on table/seat-angle.ts (labelTurnDeg, isStraight) to turn names and values upright on the 90° / 270° boards. The mock does the same with a counter-rotation per label.
  • Turn ring. table/turn-ring.tsx takes its angles from FFA4_RING_ANGLE / ringAngles(). In Arena and Spotlight the rivals are no longer at W/N/E, so the ring angles must come from each seat's real position. The mock uses atan2 from the ring to each board.
  • Spotlight is close to the existing focus camera. slotPlan() already has focus, dockL and dockR places. Spotlight would make focus the default, enlarge it (today's focus place has scale .92, which works out to roughly 66px cards at 1920; Spotlight's focus board is 81px), yaw the docks 22° instead of 90°, and keep the orbit order. The fly belongs in table/use-camera.ts and should be skipped under reduced motion, as it is in the mock.
  • Shared EMZ pairs. multi-seat.ts → sharedExtraPairs() drops a pair when either seat is eliminated. Arena and Spotlight draw the pairs in the band, so an eliminated seat must break its pair there too; the mock shows the survivor's cells as "own". multi-seat-stage.tsx is where the pairs render today.
  • Panes. table/table-shell.tsx and table-side.tsx hold the history/inspector and Master panes. The rail plus overlay drawer replaces both, and the drawer must not resize the stage, or the table would jump each time it opens.
  • Plaza. table/plaza.tsx scales the floor with the stage. With a wider stage the floor scale follows width, as in the mock (scale = stageWidth / 1650–1700).
  • Perspective at 2560. The mock scales each board's perspective() with the UI unit, so boards keep the same foreshortening at 2560. With a fixed 1500px, large boards tilt harder and the across board ran into the ring.
  • FFA3 has no shared EMZ. sharedExtraPairs() returns nothing outside FFA4, so every FFA3 seat keeps its own cells. In Arena and Spotlight FFA3, the band holds your own pair. The rivals' own cells would go back onto their boards, which the band-mode boards in the mock leave out.
  • Tag is a different stage. Real Tag runs on the Rooftop (tag/tag-stage.tsx, tag/roof-camera.ts). The Tag option in these mocks reuses the FFA4 seats with Tag tones to show team plates only. It is not a proposal for the Rooftop.

Open questions

  1. Wide: is it OK to fold the seat strip into the phase bar? It gives back 25px of height, but the tags get smaller.
  2. Arena: is it OK that the rivals no longer sit to your left and right? Turn order still reads left to right and on the ring, but "the player on my left" no longer matches the table.
  3. Spotlight: which rival should be in focus by default: the one across, the next to play, or whoever just acted? And should the spotlight follow an attack on you?
  4. Inspector: should a card click open the drawer, or show the hover holo (Wide)? Should the drawer stay pinned on wide screens (2560)?
  5. 1440: Arena rivals (50px) and Spotlight wings (39–45px) miss the 56px goal. One option is to accept that. Another is a thinner, ring-only band at 1440. That would gain a few px, but it is not mocked.
  6. Tag: where should your partner sit if Tag ever moves onto this table: across (as mocked) or beside you? Today it lives on the Rooftop.

Recommendation

Build Wide plaza first. It is the one that keeps the table as it is. Everyone stays in their seat, the shared EMZs stay on your field, and the turn ring stays between you and the player across. It still makes the side rivals' cards about 1.6× wider, or 2.5× the area (43 → 69 / 71 at 1920), and lifts your field from 72 to 78. It is also the smallest code change: a width-aware stage in geometry.ts, new home poses, corner-relative plates, and the rail and drawer in place of the panes.

Take Spotlight's camera, not its home layout. The wings straightening and growing while you pick a target is the best idea in the set, and it fits on top of the existing focus / dock camera. Ship it as what Tab does from Wide.

Arena is the alternative if size matters most. It has the biggest numbers (you 91, every rival 68 at 1920) and the cleanest band. The cost is that the table stops being a table, so choose it only if you are happy for rivals to stop sitting to your left and right.

One thing is still open. At 1440, no concept gets every rival to 56px, because height runs out first. Wide gets the most even result there (54 / 46 / 56).
