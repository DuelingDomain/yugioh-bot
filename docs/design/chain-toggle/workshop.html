Chain responses switch

A per-player switch with three states: Auto, Always, Off. It decides when the duel stops for your response windows. The opponent sees the duel pause when you are asked, so the switch is how you stop giving away that you hold a card, or bluff that you do. Only you see it, and the server applies it, so a switch never shows up as a delay on their side.

1. Where it lives

In the bottom station track, right side, just before End Turn. All three shells already mount the same StationTrack (the 1v1 room, the 3 and 4 seat table, the Rooftop), so one component with one slot covers them. It is always visible, never inside a menu, and does not move when the phase changes.

Width note. The switch adds about 190 px to the right cluster, which today is about 280 px. The real track needs its right column raised to a 520 px minimum from 1280 px up (the seat tables already do this at 380 to 520 px). Between 900 and 1279 px the "Responses" label and the key cap hide and only the three segments show. Below 900 px it becomes the third phone row.

2. Flipping while a window is open

The switch is live. Changing it applies to the next window and to one that is already open: flip to Off with a "Select a chain link or pass" tray showing and the window passes on the spot. No confirmation, no extra "Pass now" button. The same rule runs for Auto, so flipping from Always to Auto passes an open window where nothing fits.

A window that lists no card of yours passes by itself in every state. Prompts for a card's own choices (a yes/no inside an effect, choosing targets) are not response windows and are not affected.

5. Behaviour of the control

  • Keyboard. R flips between Off and your last "on" state (Auto or Always). The three segments also take Arrow keys and Space as a radio group. R is free in every shell: the table camera uses h o f s a k c p and digits, the Rooftop adds q e i v, bug marks use M, prompts use F, digits, Enter and Esc. It is ignored while typing, under a dialog, or with Ctrl, Cmd or Alt held.
  • Announce. A polite live region says "Responses: Off". Colour is never the only signal; the state is also the word.
  • Motion. A 180 ms colour change on the segments, nothing else. Nothing moves, so there is nothing for reduced motion to remove.
  • Remembered. Auto and Always are remembered in this browser and applied when a new duel starts. Off is not: it resets to the duel default, so a forgotten Off cannot carry into next week's duel.
  • Hidden for spectators, replays, finished duels, and scenario or preset duels (those are written against every window).
  • Failure. If the request fails the segment snaps back and the existing action error banner shows the message.
